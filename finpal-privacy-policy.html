Privacy Policy

FinPal
Last updated: 18 October 2026
Applies to FinPal version 1.0.3

FinPal is a personal finance app. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app stores on your device, how it is used, and what we do not collect.

In short: your data stays on your phone. FinPal v1.0.3 does not create accounts, sync to the cloud, connect to your bank, or sell your information.

We collect nothing. FinPal v1.0.3 does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The released Android app is not granted the INTERNET permission, so it has no technical ability to send your records anywhere. Everything described below is stored only on your own device.

1. Who we are

FinPal is developed by Shubham Patel (“we”, “us”, or “our”). The app helps you track income and expenses, set up recurring payments, organise transactions, and view spending summaries on your own device.

By using FinPal, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

2. What FinPal stores

Everything you enter stays on your device. FinPal v1.0.3 has no backend account system and does not upload your records to our servers.

On your device, FinPal may store:

  • Profile details — display name, gender, date of birth, monthly income if you enter one, and any profile picture you choose.
  • Transactions — income and expense entries you add, including amount, date, category, payment method, optional notes, and any receipt photo you attach.
  • Recurring series — schedules you create for repeating income or expenses, including amount, category, payment method, interval, start and end dates, status (active, paused, or completed), and optional notes. When a due date arrives, FinPal may create a matching transaction on your device.
  • Preferences — categories and payment methods you create or customise, theme and currency settings, and whether you have completed onboarding.

FinPal does not ask for a password, email login, bank credentials, or permission to access your bank accounts.

3. Camera and photos

When you choose to set a profile picture or attach a receipt, FinPal opens the system camera or photo picker. Those system screens handle access; FinPal only receives the single image you select or take. Nothing else in your gallery is read.

When you pick an image, FinPal copies it into the app’s private storage on your device so the file is not lost if the system clears temporary cache. That copy is not uploaded. It stays on your phone and is removed when you delete the profile picture, delete the related transaction, clear the app’s data, or uninstall FinPal.

FinPal does not scan, index, or read other photos on your device. It does not keep ongoing access to your gallery beyond what you select.

The released Android app declares no camera or photo/video storage permissions. Image attachment relies on the system camera and photo picker only.

FinPal does not request location, contacts, SMS, call logs, notification access, or the INTERNET permission.

4. How that information is used

Your data is used only to run FinPal on your device, including to:

  • Show balances, history, upcoming dues, and spending charts.
  • Create transactions from recurring series when their due dates arrive.
  • Personalise your profile and categories.
  • Reflect spending relative to recorded income.

We do not use your financial records for advertising, profiling across other apps, or selling lists.

5. Where your data lives

FinPal stores information locally on your phone. If you clear the app’s data or uninstall FinPal, that local information is removed from the device.

Because data stays on your phone, you control it. Protect your device with a lock screen and keep the system up to date. There is no cloud backup in FinPal v1.0.3. If you lose the device or clear the data without your own backup, the records are gone.

6. Sharing and third parties

In FinPal v1.0.3, we do not share your profile, transaction, or recurring data with third-party services. This version does not include cloud sync, analysis SDKs, crash reporters, or advertising networks that collect your FinPal records.

We do not sell your personal information. We would only disclose information if required by law, but in v1.0.3 we hold no copy of your FinPal records, so there is nothing for us to hand over.

Opening the Privacy Policy, Terms, support links, or Share hands an address or short promo text to your browser, email app, or system share sheet. Those apps then handle it. FinPal itself makes no network request. Those apps have their own privacy practices. Share sends a store link and promo text only — not your financial records.

7. Your choices

You can:

  • Edit or delete transactions, recurring series, and profile details inside the app.
  • Use Clear Data in FinPal’s Settings screen, or clear app data from your phone’s system settings.
  • Uninstall FinPal to remove locally stored information.

8. Children’s privacy

FinPal is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, contact us, and we can help you remove local data.

9. Changes

If FinPal’s data practices change in a later version (for example, optional cloud sync or other networked features), we will update this Privacy Policy and the “Last updated” date. This page describes FinPal v1.0.3.
